4. FINDINGS, IDENTIFIED PROBLEMS & IMPLEMENTED RESOLUTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[FINDING-44-01] CRITICAL (RESOLVED) — Replaced Stub Fulfillment Adapter with Official MyStars FaaS v1 Integration
- Problem: Prior to Stage 4.4, `FulfillmentService` defaulted exclusively to `ManualAdminFulfillmentAdapter`, requiring manual admin intervention for every paid Stars and Premium order.
- Fix: Implemented `MyStarsClient` and `MyStarsFulfillmentAdapter` strictly adhering to the official MyStars FaaS v1 API (`https://api.mystars.tg/v1`). When `MYSTARS_API_KEY` is configured, paid orders automatically create or poll MyStars orders with `Idempotency-Key: quizystars-order-{orderId}`.

[FINDING-44-02] HIGH (RESOLVED) — Recipient Pre-Check Before Payment / Balance Deduction
- Problem: If a buyer entered a non-existent Telegram `@username`, a bot account, or an account that already had an active Telegram Premium subscription, charging their crypto invoice or deducting their internal balance first would lead to failed fulfillment and manual refund overhead.
- Fix: Integrated `POST /v1/recipients/check` (`myStarsClient.validateRecipientBeforeCheckout`) prior to order creation, recipient update, external invoice generation, and internal balance deduction. If `eligible === false && !indeterminate`, checkout is blocked immediately with a clear Russian message. If `indeterminate === true` or Fragment/MyStars experiences a transient timeout, the check fails open as recommended by the official MyStars SDK.

[FINDING-44-03] HIGH (RESOLVED) — Authoritative Webhook Verification & 24-Hour Secret Rotation
- Problem: MyStars FaaS v1 signs webhook notifications with `X-Faas-Signature` (lowercase hex HMAC-SHA256 of raw request body) and supports a 24-hour key rotation window where two secrets may be valid simultaneously.Furthermore, webhook payloads must not be trusted blindly without authoritative state verification.
- Fix: Implemented `verifyMyStarsWebhookSignature` supporting comma-separated `"current_secret,previous_secret"` via `crypto.timingSafeEqual`, and implemented `FulfillmentService.processMyStarsWebhook` to authoritatively fetch `GET /v1/orders/{order_id}` before completing or failing the order.

[FINDING-44-04]HIGH (RESOLVED) — Rate Limit Differentiation (429 `general` vs 429 `order_cap`)
- Problem: MyStars returns HTTP 429 in two distinct cases: general rate limiting (with `RateLimit-Limit` / `Retry-After` headers, which is retryable) and `order_cap` (when 10 unpaid `awaiting_payment` orders are already open, which should not be retried immediately in a tight loop).
- Fix: `MyStarsRateLimitError` distinguishes `kind: 'general'` (`retryable: true`) from `kind: 'order_cap'` (`retryable: false`), and `FulfillmentService.initiateFulfillment` respects `error.retryable === false` to immediately mark the order for reconciliation and notify admins without wasting 3 retry loops.

[FINDING-44-05] MEDIUM (RESOLVED) — Background Reconciliation for Delayed Webhooks
- Problem: If a CryptoBot, xRocket, or MyStars webhook is delayed or dropped during a container restart, paid orders or balance top-ups could remain stuck in `pending` or `processing`.
- Fix: Extended `FulfillmentService.recoverPendingFulfillments` (executed on startup and every 60 seconds) to poll in-flight MyStars orders (`GET /v1/orders/{id}`), pending CryptoBot/xRocket order invoices (`PaymentService.reconcilePendingOrderPayments`), and pending CryptoBot/xRocket balance top-ups (`balanceService.reconcilePendingTopUps`).
